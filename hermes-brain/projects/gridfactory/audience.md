## Objections To Answer With Strength

- "Why not just buy NVIDIA?" Answer: NVIDIA sells the shovels; GridFactory targets ownership in the powered compute infrastructure that uses them.
- "Why green-energy parks?" Answer: power and grid access are the bottleneck. Energy parks already solved part of the hard problem.
- "Why modular?" Answer: staged deployment, faster connection, less capital friction, containers working together as one compute fabric.
- "Why now?" Answer: AI demand, GPU scarcity, data-center bottlenecks, Europe lagging, sovereign compute, and investor appetite are converging.
- "Is this real or just crypto?" Answer: the story is physical infrastructure first: containers, GPUs, racks, cooling, power, fiber, compute contracts.
