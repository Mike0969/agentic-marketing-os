# GridFactory — Offer

## Product / Service

GridFactory sells AI compute capacity through modular GPU data centers deployed in renewable energy parks.

The commercial engine is Compute-as-a-Service: GPU hours sold to AI startups, enterprises, governments, research institutions, inference platforms, agent workloads, robotics, autonomous systems, and sovereign AI customers.

ACRaaS deck demand language:

- sovereign AI and government AI;
- AI labs and fine-tuners;
- agentic AI and robotics;
- enterprise reserved leases;
- spot GPU demand;
- always-on inference workloads.

## Investor Offer

The investor product is the Compute Unit Grid lease-to-own model.

Investors fund hardware deployment and receive a proportional monthly share of net CaaS revenue generated by the compute capacity their investment supports. Over time, they progressively own the compute unit they funded.

Simple investor flow:

Investor buys GRID units with fiat or crypto -> GRID units link to a defined slice of TFLOPS capacity -> the container rents compute to AI customers -> net CaaS revenue is distributed monthly -> GRID units can also become compute credits for users who need AI workloads.

The business plan includes:

- $100 minimum GRID Unit linked to approximately 180 TFLOPS.
- $1,000 GRID Position linked to approximately 1,800 TFLOPS.
- $10,000 Full CUG Node linked to approximately 18,000 TFLOPS.
- Monthly scenario income examples: approximately $3.30/month, $32.80/month, and $327.50/month respectively.
- Phase 1 Container 01 base case: approximately $115,500/month net at full capacity.
- Container 01 staged raise: approximately $3.53M.
- Campus 01 base case: approximately $386,100/month net.
