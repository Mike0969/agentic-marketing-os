## Industrial Thesis

The ACRaaS deck makes the GridFactory thesis simple:

AI demand is exploding, but the true bottleneck is not only GPU supply. It is the combination of power, grid interconnection, permitting, and clustered high-speed compute capacity.

Hyperscalers validate the thesis by spending billions to secure dedicated power for AI data centers. GridFactory responds with a lighter model: already-built partner energy parks plus modular GPU containers.

## Investor Product Story

The deck frames GRID units as tokenised, lease-to-own positions linked to a defined slice of compute capacity and proportional net CaaS revenue.

Core investor flow:

Investor buys GRID units with fiat or crypto -> GRID units link to container TFLOPS capacity -> container sells compute through CaaS contracts -> net CaaS revenue flows back monthly to unit holders -> units may also be redeemed as compute credits.

Use this flow heavily in posts, carousels, diagrams, and video scripts.
