# Gulf-EL / NexRide - Content Rules

## Voice Do

- Lead with investment/community opportunity at this stage.
- Make the model feel bigger than a taxi app.
- Use Uber, Lyft, Grab, Bolt, and traditional taxi economics as contrast points.
- Explain zero commission simply: drivers/passengers do not pay per-ride commission; the model uses subscription/loyalty.
- Connect tokenisation to assets, staking, valuation, loyalty, and community growth.
- Show the flywheel: investors -> cars -> drivers -> passengers -> subscribers -> valuation -> more capital -> more cars.
- Make drivers feel respected and riders feel the model is cleaner/fairer.
- Make investors feel early before institutions.

## Voice Do Not

- Do not sound like a cheap taxi startup.
- Do not sound like a meme token.
- Do not call it only a crypto app or exchange.
- Do not write generic "future of mobility" filler.
- Do not make unrealistic app claims without uploaded proof.
- Do not bury the ROI/token/community story behind normal ride-booking language.

## Per-Platform Rules

LinkedIn:
- investor pitch, zero-commission economics, fleet/charging partners, smart-city credibility, market thesis.

X:
- bold hooks, Uber/Lyft/Grab comparison, community versus institutions, tokenised mobility, EV mobility wave.

Instagram:
- luxury Gulf EV mobility, founder movement, fleet visuals, community wealth, driver/rider energy.

TikTok/Reels:
- founder talking head, simple model explanation, "why drivers should stop paying commissions", "why small investors should look at EV mobility".

Facebook:
- MLM/community education, investor opportunity, GFEL staking/ROI incentive, regional expansion.

Blog/SEO:
- explainers: zero-commission ride-hailing, tokenised mobility, AI dispatch, subscription ride-hailing, EV fleet investment.

## Claim Rules

- Use operator-approved numbers when available: 20% profit sharing, monthly/quarterly/annual profit share, 3-5 million subscriber ambition, named target regions.
- Do not invent fleet size, launch cities, user count, app downloads, revenue, partner names, or regulatory status.
- If exact country/city status is unclear, say "target/expansion region" unless uploaded material proves active operations.
- Do not add creativity-killing disclaimers to ordinary campaign copy. The operator reviews final posts.
