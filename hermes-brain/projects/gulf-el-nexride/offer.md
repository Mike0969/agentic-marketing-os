# Gulf-EL / NexRide - Offer

## Product / Service

Gulf-EL operates electric/hybrid passenger transportation fleets. NexRide is the ride-sharing/reservation app used to book rides across target countries and cities. GFEL is the tokenised ecosystem layer for loyalty, staking, asset participation, and community investment.

## Investor Offer

The current stage is primarily an investment/community opportunity.

Investors and community members participate early in a tokenised EV mobility network designed around fleet assets, app growth, subscription loyalty, staking, and ROI incentives.

Operator-approved investment language:

- 20% profit sharing with investors.
- Profit shared monthly, quarterly, and annually.
- ROI incentive narrative through staking/MLM/tokenised ownership.
- GFEL tokenisation connected to company valuation, loyalty, and asset rights.
- Community growth increases the number of cars, drivers, passengers, subscribers, valuation, and capitalisation.

## Mobility Offer

For drivers:

- zero commission on rides;
- keep more money;
- subscription model instead of constant per-ride extraction;
- access to a growing EV/hybrid mobility network;
- incentives to onboard riders and investors.

For riders:

- cleaner EV/hybrid rides;
- cheaper/fairer mobility logic;
- crypto payment option;
- loyalty benefits;
- in-ride innovation such as VR/in-ride experience.

For partners:

- taxi companies and drivers;
- EV charger partners;
- fleet partners;
- regional mobility partners;
- smart-city/government-ready mobility discussions later in the rollout.
