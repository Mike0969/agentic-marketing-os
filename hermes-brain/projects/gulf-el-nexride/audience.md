# Gulf-EL / NexRide - Audience

## Primary Audience Now

The first market is investment/community.

- small investors;
- crypto users;
- MLM/community users;
- early mobility believers;
- people who missed early Uber/Lyft/Grab-style platform upside;
- users attracted by monthly, quarterly, and annual ROI incentive;
- people who understand token appreciation, staking, and community growth.

## Secondary Audience

- taxi companies;
- professional drivers;
- fleet partners;
- EV charger partners;
- strategic regional mobility partners;
- riders who want cleaner/fairer rides;
- GCC smart-city and clean-mobility stakeholders.

## Later Audience

After the investment/community hype and first rollout push:

- drivers;
- riders;
- regional partners;
- government/smart-city conversations;
- larger investors;
- fleet operators;
- launch-city partners.

## Jobs To Be Done

Investors want:

- early upside before institutions arrive;
- token appreciation;
- staking/ROI incentive;
- asset-linked participation;
- a real business behind the token;
- a community strong enough to push adoption.

Drivers want:

- to keep more money;
- escape commission-heavy ride-hailing;
- better loyalty and subscription model;
- more ride flow through app/community incentives.

Riders want:

- cleaner cars;
- fairer price logic;
- loyalty rewards;
- crypto payment option;
- better ride experience.

Partners want:

- serious regional growth model;
- credible fleet demand;
- EV charging demand;
- user/community adoption engine.

## Objections Agents Must Beat

- "Is this just another Uber clone?"
- "Is this just a meme token?"
- "Where is the real mobility business?"
- "Why would drivers switch?"
- "Why would riders switch?"
- "How does tokenisation connect to real assets?"
- "How does the community actually reduce CAC?"
- "What countries and cities are real versus planned?"

## Platform Priority

- Telegram/Discord/community channels: investment, GFEL, staking, ROI incentive, community power.
- X: sharp hooks against Uber/Lyft/Grab economics, tokenised mobility, zero commission.
- Instagram/TikTok/Reels: founder-led movement, EV fleet visuals, driver/rider stories, luxury Gulf mobility, community hype.
- LinkedIn: investor pitch, mobility-market thesis, partners, fleet/charging/smart-city credibility.
- Facebook: MLM/community education, investor opportunity, rider/driver adoption.
- Blog/SEO: zero-commission ride-hailing, EV mobility investment, tokenised mobility, AI dispatch, GFEL explanation.
