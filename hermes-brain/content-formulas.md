# Content formulas

Use these as reusable structures, not rigid templates.

## LinkedIn investor-grade insight

1. Market tension.
2. Why the obvious answer is incomplete.
3. Brand-specific insight.
4. Proof or proof-needed note.
5. Strategic implication.
6. CTA.

## X concise thread

1. Hook with tension.
2. Three short proof/logic points.
3. One implication.
4. CTA.

## Carousel

1. Cover: tension or promise.
2. Problem.
3. Hidden bottleneck.
4. Why now.
5. Brand approach.
6. Proof needed / credibility frame.
7. CTA.

## Short video

1. First 2 seconds: tension.
2. Problem in plain language.
3. Why it matters now.
4. Brand angle.
5. CTA.

## Blog/article

1. Search-intent title.
2. Executive summary.
3. Problem context.
4. Market/technical explanation.
5. Brand relevance.
6. Risks or proof needed.
7. CTA.
