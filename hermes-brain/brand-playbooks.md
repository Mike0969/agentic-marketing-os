# Brand Playbooks - Codex Baseline

This is the canonical human/Codex baseline before enough live metrics exist. Claude's consolidation loop may write metric-backed updates here; Codex promotes or edits the playbook when the evidence is real.

## GridFactory.io

What converts now:

GridFactory converts when content makes small investors and crypto-native audiences feel they can own part of the AI infrastructure wave before institutional capital closes the window. The strongest story is not data-center education alone. It is early ownership, monthly ROI incentive, physical AI compute assets, green-energy power access, and the first-container path into a larger network.

Rules:

1. Lead with ownership, monthly income, early access, and AI infrastructure, then prove it with green-energy parks, GPU containers, power bottlenecks, CaaS revenue, and GRID/CUG mechanics.
2. Use deck-grade language: "Own the machines that power the AI economy", "Plug into the park, not the grid", and "tokenised compute / lease-to-own infrastructure."
3. Use numbers only from approved sources: $100 GRID Unit, scenario income examples, phase/campus numbers, TFLOPS/GPU/container figures, and operator-approved business-plan material.
4. Make CTAs ownership-based: Own your GRID Unit, join the first container, enter the Compute Unit Grid, ask for the investor business plan.
5. Visuals must show physical money: containers, GPU racks, energy parks, substations, high-speed clusters, founder explanation, investor-flow diagrams.
6. Reject generic AI commentary, "future of infrastructure" filler, flat 2D images, decorative AI brains, and dry consultant CTAs.

## Gulf-EL / NexRide

What converts now:

Gulf-EL / NexRide converts when content frames the project as an investment/community opportunity first and a mobility app second. The hook is the ride-hailing wave at inception, but EV-first, tokenised, zero-commission, subscription-based, AI-optimised, and built by a community before institutions own the upside.

Rules:

1. Lead with community-owned mobility, GFEL tokenisation, staking/ROI incentive, driver-first zero commission, and early EV mobility upside.
2. Explain the flywheel simply: community/investors -> cars -> drivers -> riders -> subscribers -> valuation -> capitalisation -> more cars.
3. Compare against Uber/Lyft/Grab/Bolt economics when useful, but do not sound like a fake clone. The point is the different model: zero commission, subscription loyalty, tokenised assets, AI dispatch, and community growth.
4. Use operator-approved claims only: Dubai HQ, target/active regions, 20% investor profit-share narrative, monthly/quarterly/annual profit-share, 3-5M subscriber ambition, GFEL, AI negotiation/dispatch, VR in-ride, crypto payments.
5. CTAs must move people into the investor/community funnel: Enter the GFEL ecosystem early, ask for the investor deck, join the Gulf-EL mobility community, join the zero-commission movement.
6. Visuals must feel real and investable: GCC streets, EV/hybrid fleets, drivers with cars, riders/families, app UI connected to a ride, charging hubs, fleet control rooms, founder video, tokenised mobility diagrams.
7. Reject cheap taxi app visuals, meme-token energy, crypto exchange framing, generic car stock, unrealistic app mockups, and "download the app" as the main story at this stage.
