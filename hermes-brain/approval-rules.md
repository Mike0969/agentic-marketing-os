# Approval rules

Human approval is required before scheduled or published workflows.

## Always require approval for

- investor claims
- partnership claims
- funding claims
- technical capability claims
- regulatory claims
- launch timelines
- token/loyalty mechanics
- pricing or commission claims
- safety claims
- competitor comparisons

## Content may enter pipeline as

- idea
- brief
- draft
- visual
- approval

## Agents may not

- approve their own work
- mark content as published
- post to any social platform
- run browser automation for posting
- invent proof to pass approval

## Approval request should include

- what content is being approved
- why it matters
- claims requiring review
- recommended action
- risk level
