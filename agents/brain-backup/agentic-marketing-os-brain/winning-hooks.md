# Winning hooks

Store reusable hook skeletons that performed well or are strategically strong. Do not store copied competitor wording.

## Hook skeletons

### Infrastructure tension

`Everyone is talking about AI compute. Fewer people are asking where the power will come from.`

Best for:
- GridFactory
- LinkedIn
- investor or infrastructure audience

### Economics reset

`The next ride-hailing battle may not be about who has more riders. It may be about who fixes driver economics first.`

Best for:
- Gulf-EL / NexRide
- LinkedIn, X
- mobility and investor audience

### Hidden bottleneck

`The bottleneck is not the dashboard. It is the physical system behind it.`

Best for:
- GridFactory
- data center, power, infrastructure narratives

### Market shift

`A market changes when the old commission model stops looking inevitable.`

Best for:
- Gulf-EL / NexRide
- zero-commission positioning
