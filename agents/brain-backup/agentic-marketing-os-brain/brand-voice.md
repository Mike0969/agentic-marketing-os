# Brand voice

## GridFactory.io

Voice:
- institutional
- infrastructure-focused
- technical
- investor-grade
- boardroom-ready

Use:
- power availability
- grid constraints
- AI data-center demand
- interconnection
- reliability
- bankability
- infrastructure partners

Avoid:
- consumer slogans
- AI hype without proof
- crypto-style language
- unsupported megawatt, funding, or partnership claims

## Gulf-EL.com / NexRide

Voice:
- futuristic
- mobility-focused
- bold but credible
- GCC-aware
- commercially clear

Use:
- zero-commission ride-hailing
- driver economics
- EV mobility
- AI dispatch
- rider trust
- tokenized loyalty only when clearly explained

Avoid:
- meme-token energy
- unsupported launch claims
- vague "future of mobility" filler
- confusing Gulf-EL, NexRide, EVs, ride-hailing, and loyalty into one story
