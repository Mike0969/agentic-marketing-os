# Weak hooks

Avoid these patterns unless a human explicitly asks for them.

## Generic AI hooks

- `AI is changing everything.`
- `The future is here.`
- `Welcome to the next generation of...`

Why weak:
- too generic
- no tension
- no proof
- sounds like commodity AI marketing

## Generic EV hooks

- `Driving into a greener future.`
- `The future of mobility starts now.`
- `Join the EV revolution.`

Why weak:
- overused
- does not explain Gulf-EL / NexRide's difference
- ignores driver economics and zero commission

## Unsupported investor hooks

- `This will redefine infrastructure investing.`
- `The biggest opportunity in AI power.`
- `A guaranteed winner in mobility.`

Why weak:
- unsupported
- creates legal/credibility risk
- needs proof, source, or human approval

## Repair rule

Replace vague claims with:
- specific market tension
- proof or source requirement
- credible business implication
- clear CTA
